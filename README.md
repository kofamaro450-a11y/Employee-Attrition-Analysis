# Employee Attrition Analysis

**AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship · Business Understanding & Data Exploration**

## Project Overview

This project explores the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset for a fictional client, ABC Manufacturing Ltd. The objective is to understand employee turnover patterns and identify factors associated with attrition before investing in predictive models.

## Key Findings

- **Overall attrition:** 16.1% (237 of 1,470 employees); the dataset is imbalanced.
- **Overtime:** Employees working overtime had a 30.5% attrition rate, compared with 10.4% among those who did not.
- **Age:** Employees aged 18–25 had the highest attrition rate at 35.8%, compared with 9.2% among employees aged 36–45.
- **Monthly income:** Employees who left had a median monthly income of 3,202, compared with 5,204 among those who stayed.
- **Job role:** Sales Representatives recorded the highest attrition rate by role at 39.8%.
- **Department:** Sales had the highest departmental attrition rate at 20.6%.
- **Marital status and travel:** Single employees had an attrition rate of 25.5%, while frequent travellers had a rate of 24.9%.

> **Note:** These findings represent associations observed in a single dataset snapshot and do not establish causation.
